parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date with both parts at 12 or below comes out with day and month swapped: a 7 April booking lands on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. It covers every report from a day-first tenant that did not come in through a form showing a month-first hint, and that is not a one-day move.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date flips before the parser sees it, so the parser fix does not touch it. It covers reports from tenants on booking-v3 that came in through the form. Intake tags these by the reporter’s browser, so the hint tag and this problem disagree; the tenant decides, as in causes.ts.

one-day: the date moves one day, not a month

These reports do not describe a swap. The booking moves back by one day: Tuesday becomes Monday, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made, every June booking is one day off. A day/month swap cannot do that. The desk already marked them in desk-notes.json and counted them under parser by tenant. They are a separate problem with a separate fix, most likely in how a date is converted between time zones.
